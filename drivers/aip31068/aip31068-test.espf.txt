====================
AiP31068 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/aip31068: the project-local `<aip31068>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual AiP31068 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x3E).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on; the panel's own content is read
back with `part lcd state`.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="aip31068_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <aip31068 id="lcd" bus="$i2c0" address="0x3E" />
