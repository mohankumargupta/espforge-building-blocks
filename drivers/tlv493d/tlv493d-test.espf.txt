====================
TLV493D test firmware

Fixture for drivers/tlv493d: the project-local `<tlv493d>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual TLV493D-A1B6 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address
0x5E).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="tlv493d_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <tlv493d id="field" bus="$i2c0" address="0x5E" />
