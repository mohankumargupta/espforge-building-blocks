====================
CCS811 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/ccs811: the project-local `<ccs811>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual CCS811 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x5A). Prints
`TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ccs811_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <ccs811 id="air" bus="$i2c0" address="0x5A" />
