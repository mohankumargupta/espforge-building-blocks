====================
I2C scanner: the emulator's built-in EEPROM

The stock espforge `i2c_scanner` example on the SHT31 fixture's pads
(SDA = GPIO 5, SCL = GPIO 6), for running with NO --cosim-board. The
emulator's own I2C port is then in charge, and its built-in 24Cxx EEPROM
answers at 0x50.

A scan that finds 0x50 proves esp-hal's I2C transactions complete at all, so
a failing SHT31 fixture is the co-simulated part, not the toolchain. A scan
that finds nothing means the gate in testsht31.md §1a is still the toolchain.
