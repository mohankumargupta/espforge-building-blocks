====================
HT16K33 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/ht16k33: the project-local `<ht16k33>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual HT16K33 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x70).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ht16k33_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <ht16k33 id="display" bus="$i2c0" address="0x70" />
