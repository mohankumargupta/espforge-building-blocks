====================
AM2320 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/am2320: the project-local `<am2320>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual AM2320 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x5C). Prints
`TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="am2320_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <am2320 id="climate" bus="$i2c0" address="0x5C" />
