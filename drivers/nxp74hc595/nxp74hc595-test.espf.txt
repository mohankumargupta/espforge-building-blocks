====================
74HC595 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/nxp74hc595: the project-local, busless `<nxp74hc595>`
driver, wired to the virtual 74HC595 part (OE = GPIO 4, SER = GPIO 3,
SRCLR = GPIO 5, SRCLK = GPIO 6, RCLK = GPIO 7). The manifest's `bus "none"`
means the crate bit-bangs the five lines; this project builds no SPI
peripheral. Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
