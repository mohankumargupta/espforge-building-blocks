====================
BME280 I2C test firmware

Fixture for drivers/bme280_i2c: the project-local `<bme280_i2c>` driver over
I2C, wired to the virtual BME280 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, SDO low =
0x76). Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="bme280_i2c_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <bme280_i2c id="climate" bus="$i2c0" address="0x76" />
