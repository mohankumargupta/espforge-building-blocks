====================
MPU-9250 test firmware

Fixture for planmpu9250.md: the project-local `<mpu9250>` driver over SPI,
wired to the virtual MPU-9250 part (CS = GPIO 7, SCK = GPIO 6, MOSI = GPIO 3,
MISO = GPIO 2). Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

  <spi id="spi2" sclk="6" mosi="3" miso="2" mode="0" frequency="1000kHz" />

# devices
