====================
TMP102 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/tmp102: the project-local `<tmp102>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual TMP102 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x48).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="tmp102_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <tmp102 id="temp" bus="$i2c0" address="0x48" />
