====================
INA219 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/ina219: the project-local `<ina219>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual INA219 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x40).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ina219_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <ina219 id="power" bus="$i2c0" address="0x40" />
