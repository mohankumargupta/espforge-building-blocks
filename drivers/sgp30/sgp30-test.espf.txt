====================
SGP30 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/sgp30: the project-local `<sgp30>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual SGP30 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x58).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="sgp30_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <sgp30 id="air" bus="$i2c0" address="0x58" />
