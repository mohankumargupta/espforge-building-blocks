====================
HDC302x test firmware

Fixture for drivers/hdc302x: the project-local `<hdc302x>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual HDC302x part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x44).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="hdc302x_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <hdc302x id="climate" bus="$i2c0" address="0x44" />
