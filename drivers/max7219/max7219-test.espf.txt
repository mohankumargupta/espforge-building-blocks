====================
MAX7219 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/max7219: the project-local, busless `<max7219>` driver,
wired to the virtual MAX7219 part (DATA = GPIO 3, CS = GPIO 7, SCK = GPIO 6).
The manifest's `bus "none"` means the driver bit-bangs the three lines; this
project builds no SPI peripheral. Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
