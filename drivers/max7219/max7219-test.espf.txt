====================
MAX7219 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/max7219: the project-local `<max7219>` driver over SPI,
wired to the virtual MAX7219 part (CS = GPIO 7, SCK = GPIO 6, DIN = GPIO 3).
The display has no MISO, so the bus is write-only. Prints `TEST:` lines the
recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="max7219_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <spi id="spi2" sclk="6" mosi="3" mode="0" frequency="1000kHz" />

# devices
  <max7219 id="matrix" bus="$spi2" cs="7" />
