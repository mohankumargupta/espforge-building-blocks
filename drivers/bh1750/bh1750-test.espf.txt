====================
BH1750 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/bh1750: the project-local `<bh1750>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual BH1750 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x23). Prints
`TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="bh1750_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <bh1750 id="light" bus="$i2c0" address="0x23" />
