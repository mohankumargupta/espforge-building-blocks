====================
AS7262 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/as7262: the project-local `<as7262>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual AS7262 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x49). Prints
`TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="as7262_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <as7262 id="spectrum" bus="$i2c0" address="0x49" />
