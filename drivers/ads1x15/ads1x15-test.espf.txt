====================
ADS1115 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/ads1x15: the project-local `<ads1x15>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual ADS1115 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, ADDR low =
0x48). Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ads1x15_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <ads1x15 id="adc" bus="$i2c0" address="0x48" />
