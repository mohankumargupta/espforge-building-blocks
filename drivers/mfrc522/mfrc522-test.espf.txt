====================
MFRC522 test firmware

Fixture for planmfrc522.md: the project-local `<mfrc522>` driver over SPI,
wired to the virtual MFRC522 part (CS = GPIO 7, SCK = GPIO 6, MOSI = GPIO 3,
MISO = GPIO 2). Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

  <runtime type="blocking" />

  <spi id="spi2" sclk="6" mosi="3" miso="2" mode="0" frequency="1000kHz" />
