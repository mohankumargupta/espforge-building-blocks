====================
PCF857x test firmware

Fixture for drivers/pcf857x: the project-local `<pcf857x>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual PCF8574 part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x20).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="pcf857x_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <pcf857x id="io" bus="$i2c0" address="0x20" />
