====================
SCD4x test firmware

Fixture for drivers/scd4x: the project-local `<scd4x>` driver over I2C, wired
to the virtual SCD4x part (SCL = GPIO 6, SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x62).
Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="scd4x_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <scd4x id="co2" bus="$i2c0" address="0x62" />
