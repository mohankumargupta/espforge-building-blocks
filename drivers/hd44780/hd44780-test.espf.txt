====================
HD44780 test firmware

Fixture for drivers/hd44780: the project-local `<hd44780>` driver over I2C,
wired to the virtual PCF8574T backpack and HD44780 controller (SCL = GPIO 6,
SDA = GPIO 5, address 0x27). Prints `TEST:` lines the recipes assert on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="hd44780_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="5" scl="6" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <hd44780 id="lcd" bus="$i2c0" address="0x27" />
