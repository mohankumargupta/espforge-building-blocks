====================
MH-Z19B test firmware

Fixture for drivers/mhz19b: the `<mhz19b>` manifest driver over UART1, wired to
the virtual MH-Z19B part in drivers/mhz19b/board.json. The part's TX is on
GPIO 5 and its RX on GPIO 4, so the chip transmits on 4 and receives on 5.
Prints `TEST:` lines the happy-path recipe asserts on.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="mhz19b_test" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <uart id="uart1" uart="UART1" tx="4" rx="5" baud_rate="9600" />

# devices
  <mhz19b id="co2" bus="$uart1" />
