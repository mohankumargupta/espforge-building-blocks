====================
RMT hello

The esp-hal `rmt-hello` example as an espforge project: one raw RMT transmit
channel on GPIO 4, sending "hello\n" with 115200 8N1 timing (80 MHz / 80 =
1 MHz, 9 ticks a bit) once a second. The app supplies the PulseCodes.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="rmt_hello" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <rmt id="rmt0" />

# devices
  <rmt_tx id="uart_tx" bus="$rmt0" pin="4" clk_divider="80" idle_level="high" />
