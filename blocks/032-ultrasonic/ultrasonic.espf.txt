====================
032 - ULTRASONIC DISTANCE AND FLIGHT TIMING

Both pins are ordinary GPIO: the trigger is an <led> pulse and the echo is a
<button> input. There is no input capture, so app.rs measures the echo by
polling with delay_ns; the reading is coarse but real.
