====================
095 - DMX512 STAGE LIGHTING SERIAL UNIVERSE MASTER

The UART is real, including 250000 baud and two stop bits, and so is the
513-byte buffer. The BREAK and mark-after-break that delimit a DMX frame are
not: the UART has no break primitive, and without it a receiver ignores the
frame.
====================
