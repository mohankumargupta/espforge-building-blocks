====================
094 - MOTORIZED GATE AND DUAL-ENDSTOP INTERLOCK SAFETY COVER

No <cover> entity: two <led> relays and two limit <button>s carry the whole
safety story in app.rs, with mutually exclusive relays, a 25 s travel timeout
and an endstop that stops the motor early.
====================
