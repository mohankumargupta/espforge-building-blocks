====================
034 - MODBUS RTU AND RS485 HALF-DUPLEX ARBITRATION

UART1 and the RS485 direction pin are real. There is no <modbus> controller:
app.rs builds the request, toggles DE, and checks the CRC16 itself.

GPIO20/21 (the roadmap's UART) are UART0, the C3 console pins, so the bus moves
to GPIO0/1.
