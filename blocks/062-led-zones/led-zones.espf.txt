====================
062 - ADDRESSABLE LED STRIP PARTITIONING AND VIRTUAL ZONES

<led_strip> and <light_zone> are proposals: no RMT channel, no strip driver and
no shared framebuffer. The zones are a compiler problem (non-overlapping index
ranges) as much as a runtime one.
====================
