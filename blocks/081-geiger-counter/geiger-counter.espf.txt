====================
081 - RADIATION PULSE COUNTER AND GEIGER-MULLER TUBE (CPM TO uSv/h)

No <pulse_counter> and no PCNT: the tube output is a pulled-up <button> sampled
every millisecond, counted over a 10 s window and scaled to CPM. A 100 us pulse
can slip between samples; a hardware counter would not.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="geiger_counter" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <gpio id="gpio4" pin="4" direction="input" />

# devices
  <button id="geiger_pulse" gpio="$gpio4" pull_up="true" />
