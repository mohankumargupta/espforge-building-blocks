====================
002 - WIFI AND OTA

Wi-Fi is real; <ota> is the proposed element for this block. Remove that line
to build the Wi-Fi half with espforge today.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="wifi_ota" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
  <ota id="ota" password="admin" />
