====================
015 - ADDRESSABLE RGB LED STRIP (RMT ENGINE)

<ws2812> is a proposal: there is no RMT peripheral element and no <light>
device. The pin is declared like an <led> pin so its direction is checked.
====================

  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <gpio id="gpio3" pin="3" direction="output" />

# devices
