====================
074 - LASER PARTICULATE AIR QUALITY SENSOR (PMS5003)

No <pmsx003> driver: UART1 is real and app.rs syncs on the 0x424D magic,
checks the 30-byte sum, and decodes the three atmospheric concentrations from
the 32-byte frame. GPIO20/21 are UART0, so the sensor moves to GPIO0/1.
