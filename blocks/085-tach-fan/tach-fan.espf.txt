====================
085 - 4-WIRE TACHOMETER FAN AND CLOSED-LOOP RPM SENSING

<pwm> and <pcnt> are both proposals. The drive needs 25 kHz and the tach is
100 Hz at 3000 RPM, so neither software timing nor polling can substitute.
The two inputs/outputs are ordinary C3 pins.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="tach_fan" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <pcnt id="tach_counter" pin="7" pull_up="true" />

# devices
  <pwm id="fan_drive" pin="6" frequency="25kHz" resolution="8bit" />
  <speed_fan id="chassis" output="$fan_drive" tach="$tach_counter" />
