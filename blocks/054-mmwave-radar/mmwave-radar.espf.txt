====================
054 - 24 GHZ MMWAVE RADAR AND MICRO-MOTION STREAM PARSING

No <ld2410> driver: UART1 is real and app.rs hunts the F4F3F2F1 header,
checks the length and tail, and decodes the target state byte. The roadmap's
GPIO20/21 are UART0 (the console), so the radar moves to GPIO0/1.
