====================
066 - BATTERY-BACKED I2C REAL-TIME CLOCK (DS3231)

No <ds3231> time source: the bus is real and app.rs reads the BCD registers
directly and decodes them. Runs with no Wi-Fi at all, which is the point of an
RTC next to block 011's SNTP.
