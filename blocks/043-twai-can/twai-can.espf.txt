====================
043 - TWAI / CAN BUS CONTROLLER AND IDENTIFIER FILTERING

<twai> and <can_listener> are proposals: the C3's ISO 11898 controller is not
modelled, so there is no bit timing, no acceptance mask and no frame API.
The relay on the receiving side is a real <led>.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="twai_can" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <twai id="can" tx="6" rx="7" bitrate="500kbps" />

# devices
  <led id="auxiliary_relay" pin="10" />

# services
  <can_listener id="aux_frames" can="$can" can_id="0x200" />
