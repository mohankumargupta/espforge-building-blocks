====================
047 - 5-CHANNEL RGBWW AND COLOR TEMPERATURE BLENDING

<pwm> and <light> are proposals: there is no LEDC output and no light entity,
so five channels and their mireds blend have nowhere to go. app.rs holds the
real part, the RGB-to-RGBWW and gamma arithmetic.
====================
