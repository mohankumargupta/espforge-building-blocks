====================
084 - MULTI-ZONE SPRINKLER AND IRRIGATION VALVE CONTROLLER

No <sprinkler> entity: three <led> relays sequence the zones in app.rs, with
one zone open at a time and a master valve that follows. Cycled by a countdown
so the block finishes a full 45 minute run without blocking.
====================
