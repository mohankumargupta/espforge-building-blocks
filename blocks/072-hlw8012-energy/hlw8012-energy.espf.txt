====================
072 - DUAL-MODE FREQUENCY ENERGY MONITOR (HLW8012 / BL0937)

The three pins are ordinary GPIO, but mains power means a CF frequency in the
kilohertz: polling cannot count it. <pcnt> is the missing primitive, shared
with blocks 017, 081 and 085. The SEL toggle is real.
====================
