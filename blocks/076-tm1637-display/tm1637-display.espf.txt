====================
076 - 7-SEGMENT 4-DIGIT SERIAL DISPLAY (TM1637)

No <tm1637> driver: CLK and DIO are <led> outputs and app.rs bit-bangs the
start/stop conditions and the LSB-first bytes. Two <led>s are write-only, so
the acknowledge bit is skipped.
