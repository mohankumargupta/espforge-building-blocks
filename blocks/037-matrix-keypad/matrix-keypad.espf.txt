====================
037 - MATRIX KEYPAD SCANNING AND ANTI-GHOSTING

Rows are <led> outputs driven low in turn, columns are pulled-up <button>
inputs. app.rs scans the 2x2 grid and locks out a second key until release.
GPIO2 is a C3 strapping pin, hence the expected check warning.
