071 - 6-AXIS INERTIAL MEASUREMENT UNIT (MPU-6050)

No <mpu6050> driver: the bus is real and app.rs does the 14-byte burst read
from 0x3B, converts the signed 16-bit pairs with the datasheet factors, and
writes the digital low-pass filter register once.
