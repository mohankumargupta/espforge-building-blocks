====================
092 - HIGH-PRECISION PLATINUM RTD TEMPERATURE (MAX31865)

No <max31865> driver: the SPI bus is real and app.rs reads the 15-bit RTD
register, checks the fault bit, and runs the Callendar-Van Dusen conversion
for a PT100. SPI mode 1 is required by the part.
