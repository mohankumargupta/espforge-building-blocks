====================
061 - NMEA 0183 GPS POSITIONING AND SENTENCE DECODING

No <gps> driver: UART1 is real and app.rs reads lines, verifies the NMEA XOR
checksum, and decodes $GPRMC position plus $GPGGA altitude and satellites.
GPIO20/21 are UART0 (the console), so the module moves to GPIO0/1.
