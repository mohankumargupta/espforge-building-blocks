====================
086 - I2C CHARACTER LCD MATRIX (HD44780 + PCF8574 BACKPACK)

No <lcd_pcf8574> driver: the bus is real and app.rs drives the backpack's
enable strobe by hand, four bits at a time. The temperature line reads a real
<scd4x>.
====================

  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="4" scl="5" frequency="100kHz" />

# devices
  <scd4x id="air" bus="$i2c0" address="0x62" />
