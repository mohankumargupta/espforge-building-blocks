====================
070 - UDP SYSLOG REMOTE DIAGNOSTIC STREAMING

<syslog> is a proposal. Two things are missing: a UDP socket and a logger sink
that duplicates records next to esp-println. <logger> already owns filtering,
so the sink belongs there rather than in a separate service.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="syslog" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />
  <logger level="debug" network="warn" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices
# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
  <syslog id="collector" network="$home" host="192.168.1.50" port="514" facility="LOCAL0" />
