051 - PID FEEDBACK LOOP AND PRECISION TEMPERATURE REGULATION

No <pwm> and no <climate>: the PID loop runs in app.rs over a real <scd4x>
reading, with clamping and anti-windup, but the duty cycle has no output to
drive. <pwm id="heater" /> is a proposal.
