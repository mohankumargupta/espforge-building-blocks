====================
030 - SYSTEM HEALTH DIAGNOSTICS AND HARDWARE WATCHDOG

<diagnostics> and <restart_button> are proposals. The app can wait for Wi-Fi and
log, but it cannot read RSSI, heap use or uptime, and it cannot reset the chip.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="diagnostics" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices
  <restart_button id="restart_node" />

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
  <diagnostics id="health" network="$home" />
