====================
073 - H-BRIDGE DC MOTOR ACTUATOR AND DYNAMIC BRAKING

Both <pwm> outputs and <hbridge> are proposals. There is no LEDC and no bridge
driver, so the dead-time interlock and the decay mode have nowhere to live; a
pair of <led> pins could only do full speed and brake.
====================
