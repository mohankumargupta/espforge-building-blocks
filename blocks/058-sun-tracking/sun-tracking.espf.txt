====================
058 - ASTRONOMICAL SUN TRACKING AND CELESTIAL TRIGGERS

No <sun> element: <ntp> supplies UTC and app.rs does the solar geometry, using
an elevation of -4 degrees (civil twilight) as the sunset event. The porch
light is a real <led>.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="sun_tracking" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices
  <led id="porch_light" pin="3" />

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
  <ntp id="clock" network="$home" />
