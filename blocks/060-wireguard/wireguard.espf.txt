====================
060 - WIREGUARD ENCRYPTED VPN OVERLAY TUNNEL

<wireguard> is a proposal, and it needs two things the runtime lacks: a UDP
socket to handshake over and the Curve25519/ChaCha20-Poly1305 crypto. Keys
would be sourced like <wifi_station password>, never written literally.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="wireguard" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices
# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
  <wireguard id="tunnel" network="$home" address="10.0.0.15" peer="vpn.myhome.net:51820" allowed_ips="10.0.0.0/24" private_key="env:WG_PRIVATE_KEY" peer_public_key="env:WG_PEER_KEY" />
