====================
079 - MULTICAST DNS (MDNS) AND CUSTOM ZEROCONF DISCOVERY

<mdns> and <mdns_service> are proposals. The network stack has multicast and
UDP, so this is runtime work: a socket on 5353 joined to 224.0.0.251, plus a
DNS message encoder and a DNS-SD registry.
