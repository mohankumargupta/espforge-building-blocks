====================
044 - STEPPER MOTOR AND TRAPEZOIDAL ACCELERATION PROFILING

The two A4988 pins are ordinary GPIO: STEP is an <led> pulse, DIR is a level.
app.rs computes the trapezoid and times each pulse with delay_ns, so the move
runs in the foreground. A hardware pulse engine is still a proposal.
