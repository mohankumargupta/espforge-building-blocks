====================
091 - CONTACTLESS MAGNETIC ROTARY ANGLE ENCODER (AS5600)

No <as5600> driver: the bus is real and app.rs reads the 12-bit raw angle from
0x0E/0x0F and the magnet-status register, then converts counts to degrees.
