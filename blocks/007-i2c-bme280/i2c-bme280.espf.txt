====================
007 - I2C BME280

One bus, one part, several readings: `<bme280_i2c>` is a project-local driver
(see drivers/bme280_i2c.kdl), the same shape ESPHome's `sensor: platform:
bme280` gives with per-metric children. The driver crate beside this file is a
copy of drivers/bme280_i2c's, so this block builds on its own.
====================
