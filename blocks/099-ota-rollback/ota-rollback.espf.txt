====================
099 - FIRMWARE SELF-DIAGNOSTIC AND AUTOMATED OTA ROLLBACK

<ota /> is real: it generates the ota_0/ota_1 partition table, and
ctx.ota.mark_app_valid() confirms the running slot. The health check that
precedes the confirmation is the interesting part, and <wifi_station> supplies
it.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ota_rollback" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />
  <ota rollback="true" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices
  <led id="status_led" pin="3" />

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
