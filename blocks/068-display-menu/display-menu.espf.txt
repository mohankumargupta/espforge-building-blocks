====================
068 - INTERACTIVE DISPLAY MENU NAVIGATION ENGINE

<display_menu> is a proposal, but everything it drives is real: an <ssd1306>
framebuffer, two menu <button> inputs, an <led> backlight and <ota />'s
reboot. The cursor and the redraw are ordinary Rust in app.rs.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="display_menu" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />
  <ota />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="4" scl="5" frequency="400kHz" />

# devices
  <ssd1306 id="oled" bus="$i2c0" address="0x3C" size="128x64" />
  <button id="menu_next" pin="4" pull_up="true" />
  <button id="menu_enter" pin="5" pull_up="true" />
  <led id="screen_backlight" pin="10" />
