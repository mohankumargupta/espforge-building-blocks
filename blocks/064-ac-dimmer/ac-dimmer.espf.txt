====================
064 - AC PHASE-CUTTING DIMMER AND ZERO-CROSS TRIAC TIMING

<ac_dimmer> is a proposal. A late gate strobe is flicker; an early one fires
before commutation. Neither timestamped zero-cross capture nor a microsecond
one-shot exists, so unlike the servo in block 021 software timing is no
substitute here.
