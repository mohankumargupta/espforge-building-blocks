## Phase 36: Precision Angle, Thermal & Optical Instrumentation

### 91. Contactless Magnetic Rotary Angle Encoder (AS5600)
**Goal:** 12-bit contactless Hall-sensor angle decoding over I²C (4096 counts per revolution), raw angle register fusion, zero-position calibration, and magnetic field gain tracking.
```yaml
sensor:
  - platform: as5600
    name: "Weather Vane Direction"
    i2c_id: i2c0
    address: 0x36
    update_interval: 200ms
```

### 92. High-Precision Platinum RTD Temperature (MAX31865)
**Goal:** 15-bit delta-sigma ADC reading across a PT100/PT1000 platinum RTD bridge via SPI, 2/3/4-wire configuration handling, Callendar-Van Dusen equation linearization, and hardware fault status alerting.
```yaml
spi:
  clk_pin: GPIO6
  miso_pin: GPIO2
  mosi_pin: GPIO7

sensor:
  - platform: max31865
    name: "High-Temp Flue Probe"
    cs_pin: GPIO10
    reference_resistor: 430.0 ohm
    rtd_nominal_resistance: 100.0 ohm
    rtd_wires: 3
    update_interval: 2s
```

### 98. Multi-Stage Factory Reset & NVS Wipe Recovery
**Goal:** Multi-tiered physical button hold timer (hold 5s for soft restart, hold 15s for full NVS/credential flash partition formatting) paired with visual feedback cadence.
```yaml
binary_sensor:
  - platform: gpio
    pin:
      number: GPIO9
      mode: INPUT_PULLUP
      inverted: true
    name: "Physical Recovery Button"
    on_click:
      - min_length: 5s
        max_length: 14s
        then:
          - logger.log: "Soft reboot initiated"
          - button.press: restart_btn
      - min_length: 15s
        then:
          - logger.log: "Factory reset triggered: wiping NVS flash"
          - lambda: |-
              nvs_flash_erase();
              esp_restart();
```
