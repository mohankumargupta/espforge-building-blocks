====================
002 - WIFI AND OTA (TEST-ONLY V1)

A variant of blocks/002-wifi-ota used by testota.md §4.3. It keeps the same
<ota /> and <wifi_station>, adds the <http_client> transport and logs with a
TEST: prefix. There is deliberately no mark_app_valid() call, so T3 can show
the bootloader rolling back into ota_0.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="wifi_ota_v1" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />
  <ota flash_size="4MB" rollback="true" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="MyNetwork" password="MyPassword" />
  <http_client id="http" network="$home" rx_buffer_size="4096" timeout_ms="30000" />
